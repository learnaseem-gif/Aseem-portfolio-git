About the project

A moving company in Auckland, New Zealand, referred by a past contact. Nothing existed before — no brand, no website, no online presence. I designed the identity (logo, colour system, business cards and stationery) and the website, directed the build, and set up the Google Business Profile and Facebook Business page.

Four years on, the site is still live and still generating enquiries, and the profile is still collecting reviews.
