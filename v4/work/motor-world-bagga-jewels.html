About the project

Motor World Auto's was a used car dealership in the Lower Mainland specialising in vehicle financing. Bagga Jewels, owned by the same person, sold traditional Indian gold and silver jewelry. Neither business had a website when I started.

I ran all digital marketing for both businesses in-house for three years. I designed both websites in Figma and directed a Shopify developer through build and launch, built and managed both social accounts, and shot 100% of the content — five to six vehicles per shoot day at roughly twenty photos and a video each, plus full product and macro coverage for the jewelry. I also ran Meta lead-generation campaigns for the dealership.

Bagga Jewels went from in-store-only to shipping orders Canada-wide within about six months, from zero online presence. At the dealership, walk-in customers regularly cited the videos as the reason they came in.

Both businesses closed in late 2024 for personal reasons.
