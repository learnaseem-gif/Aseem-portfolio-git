About the project

Motor World Auto's was a used car dealership in the Lower Mainland specialising in vehicle financing. It had no website when I started.

I ran the dealership's digital marketing in-house for three years: designed the website in Figma and directed a Shopify developer through build and launch, set up and managed the social accounts, and ran Meta lead-generation campaigns. A shoot day meant five or six vehicles at roughly twenty photos and a video each — all shot and cut by me.

Walk-in customers regularly cited the videos as the reason they came in.

I ran this alongside Bagga Jewels, owned by the same person. The dealership closed in late 2024 for personal reasons.
