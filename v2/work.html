2021–2024

Motor World Auto's / Bagga Jewels

Automotive · Jewelry Digital in-house Photography & video

Motor World Auto's was a used car dealership in the Lower Mainland specialising in vehicle financing. Bagga Jewels, owned by the same person, sold traditional Indian silver and gold jewelry. Neither had a website when I started.

I ran their digital marketing in-house for three years. I designed both sites in Figma and worked with a Shopify developer to build them, set up and ran their social accounts, shot all the content, and ran Meta ad campaigns for leads. A dealership shoot day meant five or six cars, around twenty photos and a video each. For the jewelry, full product coverage including macro work for the website.

Bagga Jewels went from selling entirely in-store to taking orders across Canada within about six months. At the dealership, walk-ins regularly said they'd seen the videos online before coming in, and a good number became repeat customers.

Both businesses closed in late 2024 for personal reasons.
